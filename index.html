How the calculation works

Each point on the work plane gets direct light from every fixture it can "see" (walls block light). Brightness falls off with distance (inverse-square and cosine laws), and the beam angle sets how concentrated the light is.

Light bouncing off walls and ceiling is estimated from the room size and surface colour. The total is then multiplied by the maintenance factor to allow for dirt and ageing.

In 3D, the floor shows the work-plane result and each wall shows the light falling on it, calculated the same way for its height and direction.

Results are indicative and good for early design decisions. For final sign-off, use the manufacturer's photometric files (IES/LDT) in DIALux or Relux.
